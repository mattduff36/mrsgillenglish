# Mrs Gill English design

Reading: education landing for parents and pupils, classical-academic literary identity from the channel artwork. Not a SaaS tutor marketplace.

Dials: variance 4, motion 2, density 5. Trust and readability outrank spectacle.

## World

A parchment classroom desk: taupe field, cream paper, navy ink, warm brass metal, illustrated portrait in a high-collared blouse and navy jacket.

## Tokens

Use CSS variables from `src/app/globals.css`. Do not invent a second palette.

- Page: parchment `#F4EFE8` / `#F0E6DD`
- Field: taupe `#C2B4A7`
- Ink: `#161012`
- Navy (jacket sample): `#2C3849`
- Brass decorative: `#A48B71` (fails AA on parchment; never small text)
- Brass functional: `#6B5344`

## Type

- Display: Literata
- Body/UI: Source Sans 3
- Measure about 65ch. Headlines stay readable, not poster-huge.

## Motifs

Use one or two, not a classroom sticker pack: book spines, a quill, a thin brass rule, a parchment panel. The profile portrait is the human mark. Do not paste the raw YouTube banner across the hero.

## Refuse

Generic purple gradients, glass cards, fake metrics, three identical icon cards as the page structure, Inter/Geist as the voice of the site, guaranteed-grade marketing.
