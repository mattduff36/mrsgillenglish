## Broader practice (informing, not copying)

UK tutoring sites convert on trust, clarity of stage (KS3/GCSE), and a real next step. DfE-aligned accessibility practice is WCAG AA. Tutor-marketing articles push location pages and DBS badges. Those were not used: location and DBS are unknown, and fabricating local SEO pages would be dishonest.
